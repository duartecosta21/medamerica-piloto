    I

    Somewhere else, someone is searching for exactly what you do.

    Every year, millions of U.S. residents travel abroad for medical care, and Mexico and South America are among their frequent destinations.1

    They choose from a distance: what they can find, understand and trust online, in their own language. For a practice in Latin America, that demand already exists. It needs a way to reach you.

    II

    And most of them never reach you.

    Today an international patient finds a practice by chance: a referral, a post, a directory. When they write, it is often in another language, after hours, with questions nobody on the team owns.

    Without a route, interest dies between the first message and the first appointment.

    There is a route.
